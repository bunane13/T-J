How It All Began

A little story about two people who found each other.

It started with Joe`s birthday celebration in 2023, through a mutual friend, and a meeting that neither of us knew would become something so special.

Somewhere along the way, conversations became memories, friendship became love, and two individual journeys began turning into one.

On 7 November 2024, our relationship officially began. Since then, we have grown, learned, laughed, prayed, travelled, and continued choosing each other.

And on 24 September 2026, we took another beautiful step towards forever. Joe bent the knee and we got engaged!

This is our little corner of the world, a place to celebrate where we started, where we are, and everything still waiting for us.

Here's to us. Always. ♡
